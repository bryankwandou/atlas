## 1. Keyword Intent Classification Framework

| Intent Category | Buyer Psychology | Search Trigger Examples | Target Landing Surface |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transactional** | Buyer has budget and wants an immediate solution or implementation team. | "jasa automasi bisnis", "automation implementation service", "jasa workflow automation jakarta" | `/services/implementation-sprint`, `/services/automation-audit` |
| **Commercial Investigation** | Comparing solutions, evaluating features, weighing risk of AI vs human control. | "marketing agency workflow automation", "lead qualification automation software", "otomatisasi lead masuk" | `/solutions/marketing-agencies`, `/solutions/sales-operations` |

| **Informational** | Researching operational bottlenecks, speed-to-lead benchmarks, or technical safeguards. | "cara otomatisasi follow up lead whatsapp", "speed to lead statistics", "human in the loop approval architecture" | `/resources/*`, Homepage educational sections |
