# Atlas 90-Day Organic Content Engine & Editorial Roadmap

## 1. Editorial Strategy & Philosophy
In accordance with Google's Helpful Content System, every piece of content produced under this roadmap must provide original technical insight, quantifiable business metrics, or runnable code/DAG architectures. We reject generic AI-generated filler articles in favor of authoritative teardowns of actual operational bottlenecks, speed-to-lead benchmarks, and human-in-the-loop governance practices.
