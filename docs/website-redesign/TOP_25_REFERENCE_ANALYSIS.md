## 3. Design Pattern Matrix & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### Design Patterns to Adopt for Atlas
1. **The Hero Outcome Split**:
   - Left Column: Strong outcome headline, scope-defined sprint offer, dual primary/secondary CTAs, and trust strip.
   - Right Column: Interactive Lead Qualification Workflow Canvas demonstrating real step transitions (Input -> Score -> Approval -> Dispatch).
2. **The Bento Grid Problem/Solution**:
   - Contrast fragile custom scripts and chaotic spreadsheets against Atlas Supervised Workflows with human guardrails.
3. **The 5-Day Sprint Transparency Card**:
   - Detail Day 1 through Day 5 deliverables with exact pricing (Rp7.500.000) and clear scope boundaries.
4. **Interactive ROI Calculator**:
   - Sliders for lead volume and hourly cost that calculate monthly payroll hours saved and revenue protected.
5. **The Human Approval Drawer**:
   - A dedicated UI preview showing the exact review screen where team members approve WhatsApp drafts before dispatch.

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid (Strictly Prohibited)
1. **Generic AI Clichés**:
   - No purple-to-blue neon gradients.
   - No floating 3D spheres, glowing brains, or robot hands.
   - No empty headlines like "Unleash the Power of AI for Your Business".
2. **Fabricated Social Proof**:
   - Never display fake Fortune 500 logos, stock photo testimonials, or unsubstantiated "99.9% ROI" statistics.
3. **Dashboard Masking**:
   - Do not display an internal app dashboard as the landing page; visitors need a client-facing showcase that sells outcomes.
4. **Hidden Scope & Deceptive Pricing**:
   - Avoid vague "Contact Us for Custom Pricing" when a standardized 5-day sprint (Rp7.5M) is available.
5. **Simulated Actions Masquerading as Live**:
   - Never claim WhatsApp messages are live if they run in a sandbox; label sandbox simulations with absolute honesty.
