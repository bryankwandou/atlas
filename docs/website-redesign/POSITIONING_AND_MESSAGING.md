## 2. Target Buyer Segmentation

### Persona 1: Marketing Agency Owner & Managing Director
- **Primary Pain Point**: Inbound leads from paid ad campaigns sit idle in spreadsheets or email inboxes for hours. By the time a sales rep reaches out, the prospective client has hired a competitor.
- **Fear / Objection**: "If AI hallucinates or sends an embarrassing automated message to a high-ticket prospect, our agency's reputation is destroyed."
- **Atlas Resolution**: Strict Human Approval Gate. The AI analyzes, scores, and prepares the draft, but no WhatsApp or email leaves the building until an agency staff member clicks "Approve".
- **Economic Trigger**: Closing just one additional high-ticket retainer client per month (Rp15M–Rp30M) yields an immediate 2x–4x return on the implementation sprint.

### Persona 2: Head of Operations / RevOps
- **Primary Pain Point**: Operations staff spend 15+ hours weekly manually copying lead details between forms, WhatsApp Web, Google Sheets, and CRM pipelines.
- **Fear / Objection**: "Our team already tried Zapier and Make, but the workflows broke silently whenever someone changed a spreadsheet column or an API had a timeout."
- **Atlas Resolution**: Type-safe Zod schema validation, durable PostgreSQL persistence, atomic retry logic, and comprehensive audit logs.
- **Economic Trigger**: Reclaiming 60+ staff hours per month and preventing dropped leads.

### Persona 3: Sales Operations & Inbound Sales Leads
- **Primary Pain Point**: Sales reps waste 50% of their day manually qualifying budget, timeline, and fit for low-intent inquiries instead of talking to ready-to-buy decision-makers.
- **Fear / Objection**: "We don't want a generic chatbot talking to our executive clients."
- **Atlas Resolution**: Deterministic scoring rules (budget >= threshold, timeline verified, decision-maker confirmed) combined with personalized draft creation for instant human review.
- **Economic Trigger**: Increasing lead response velocity from 4 hours to under 60 seconds, which increases qualification rates by up to 391%.

## 3. Commercial Offer Architecture

### Offer Tier 1: 30-Minute Business Workflow Audit (Free Consultation)
- **Objective**: Low-friction commercial entry point for qualified business decision-makers.
- **Deliverables**:
  1. Live review of the client's current inbound lead or back-office process.
  2. Identification of manual bottlenecks and lead leakage points.
  3. Custom Workflow Blueprint mapping Triggers, AI Reasoning, and Human Approval checkpoints.
  4. Feasibility assessment showing estimated ROI and time-to-deployment.
- **Call to Action**: "Jadwalkan Workflow Audit 30 Menit" / "Book a 30-Minute Workflow Audit".

### Offer Tier 2: 5-Day Done-For-You Implementation Sprint (Rp7.500.000)
- **Objective**: Core commercial validation offer targeting 4 initial agency/SME clients.
- **Timeline & Milestones**:
  - **Day 1: Discovery & Architecture Alignment** — Confirm exact form triggers, qualification scoring rubric, and team approval roles.
  - **Day 2: Template Selection & Schema Customization** — Adapt the verified Atlas template, define Zod payload schemas, and configure AI reasoning criteria.
  - **Day 3: Channel Integration & Verification** — Connect lead intake forms, webhook receptors, CRM pipelines, and notification dispatchers.
  - **Day 4: Sandbox Testing & Human Approval Review** — Run 20+ synthetic test scenarios, verify boundary scores, test approval drawers, and ensure idempotency.
  - **Day 5: Production Deployment, Handover & Training** — Connect live channels, train the client's operations team on the approval console, and provide complete documentation.
- **Explicit Scope Boundaries**:
  - Included: 1 primary end-to-end workflow (e.g., Inbound Lead Qualification to WhatsApp/CRM), up to 3 integration endpoints, human approval drawer, audit logging, and 14 days of post-launch monitoring.
  - Excluded: Custom third-party ERP development, custom LLM fine-tuning, or more than 1 primary business process per sprint.

### Offer Tier 3: Supervised Workflow SaaS Workspace
- **Objective**: Ongoing platform access for clients who want to run multiple workflows, monitor daily execution traces, or build custom workflows in-house using the 120-template library.
- **Tiers**:
  - Free Developer Sandbox (Simulation mode, 100 test runs, mock integrations).
  - Starter Workspace (Rp450.000/mo — up to 3 active workflows, durable Postgres, human approval).
  - Growth Workspace (Rp1.500.000/mo — up to 10 active workflows, multi-seat approval queues).
  - Agency Partner (Rp3.500.000/mo — multi-client sub-workspaces, white-label approval drawer).
